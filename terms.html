Legal & Passenger Rights

Terms of Service & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Effective on all RK BUS verified coach bookings.

Standard Cancellation & Refund Tiers

All ticket cancellations are processed automatically via your traveler dashboard. Refunds are credited to the original payment method without hassle according to the following schedule:

Cancellation Time Window Refund Percentage Deduction / Processing Fee Processing Timeline
24+ Hours Before Scheduled Departure 90% Refund 10% standard fee Instant / 1-3 Business Days
12 to 24 Hours Before Departure 50% Refund 50% cancellation fee Instant / 1-3 Business Days
Under 12 Hours Before Departure 0% Refund 100% non-refundable N/A

* Note: Optional travel insurance fees and convenience fees (₹40.00) are non-refundable once the booking is confirmed.

Baggage Allowances & Restrictions

Every passenger is entitled to 1 standard check-in suitcase or duffel bag not exceeding 50 lbs (23 kg) to be stowed in the undercarriage luggage bay. In addition, one small personal carry-on (backpack, purse, or laptop bag) fitting under the seat or in the overhead rack is permitted free of charge.

Hazardous items, firearms without documentation, and flammable materials are strictly prohibited.

Boarding & Identification Rules

Passengers must arrive at the designated departure terminal or bay at least 15 minutes before the departure time. Boarding gates close 5 minutes prior to scheduled departure.

A valid government-issued photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, or passport) matching the passenger name on the digital boarding pass must be presented during QR scan.

Ladies Reserved Seats Policy

Seats marked as "Ladies Only" are reserved specifically for solo female travelers to ensure privacy and comfort on overnight sleeper coaches. Male passengers cannot be seated next to a female traveler in designated double berths unless traveling as an accompanied family unit.
