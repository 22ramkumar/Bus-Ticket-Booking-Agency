Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select sleeper berths vs regular seats?

When you click "Select Seats" on any sleeper coach in our search results, you will see separate tabs for Lower Deck and Upper Deck. Single berths are located on the left window side, while double berths are on the right side. You can click on any available berth to view its price and reserve it instantly.

What is the ticket cancellation and refund policy?

Our refund policy is 100% transparent: If cancelled 24+ hours before departure, you receive a 90% refund. If cancelled between 12 and 24 hours before departure, you receive a 50% refund. Cancellations under 12 hours receive no refund. All refunds are initiated immediately to your payment source.

Do I need to print my bus ticket?

No! You can show your digital e-ticket on your smartphone along with a valid government ID or driver's license. The driver or conductor will scan your QR code directly at the boarding gate.

What are the baggage allowance guidelines?

Each ticket includes 1 check-in bag up to 50 lbs (23 kg) in the lower luggage bay plus 1 small carry-on backpack or laptop bag inside the passenger cabin. Extra luggage can be coordinated with the operator at the terminal.
